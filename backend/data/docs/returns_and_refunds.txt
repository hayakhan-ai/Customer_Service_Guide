RETURNS AND REFUNDS - CUSTOMER SUPPORT GUIDE

Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with all tags attached. Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.

Q: How do I start a return?
A: Visit our website and go to "My Orders." Select the order and item you wish to return, choose a reason, and submit the request. You will receive a prepaid return shipping label via email within 24 hours.

Q: How long does it take to get my refund?
A: Once we receive and inspect the returned item, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. The amount will be credited back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it may take an additional 3–7 days to appear on your statement.

Q: Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
A: Yes. During the return process, select "Exchange" instead of "Refund." Choose the replacement size or color and we will ship it once we receive the original item. Exchanges are free of charge.

Q: What if I received a damaged or wrong item?
A: We sincerely apologize. Please contact us at support@ourstore.com within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund immediately, no return required.

Q: Do I have to pay for return shipping?
A: No. We provide a free prepaid return label for all eligible returns within the country. For international returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.

Q: Can I return a gift?
A: Yes. Gift returns receive store credit equal to the item's value. You will need the order number from the gift receipt or the email address of the person who placed the order.

Q: What items cannot be returned?
A: The following items are non-returnable: underwear and swimwear for hygiene reasons, digital products and gift cards, perishable goods, and any item marked as final sale at the time of purchase.
