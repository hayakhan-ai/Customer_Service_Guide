PRODUCTS AND INVENTORY - CUSTOMER SUPPORT GUIDE

Q: How do I find the right size?
A: Each product page includes a size guide specific to that item. Click "Size Guide" near the size selector. If you are between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up. You can also contact support and we will help you choose the best fit.

Q: Is the product I want back in stock?
A: On the product page, click "Notify Me When Available" and enter your email. We will alert you as soon as the item is restocked. Restocking times vary by product and supplier.

Q: Are your product photos accurate?
A: We do our best to represent colors accurately, but slight variations may occur due to different screen settings. If color accuracy is important, refer to the product description for specific color details.

Q: Do your products come with a warranty?
A: Warranty coverage depends on the product category. Electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Clothing and accessories do not include a warranty but are covered by our 30-day return policy. Warranty details are listed on each product page.

Q: Can I buy a product in bulk or wholesale?
A: Yes. For bulk orders of 20 or more units, please contact our wholesale team at wholesale@ourstore.com. We offer special pricing and dedicated support for business customers.

Q: How do I know if a product is authentic?
A: All products sold on our store are sourced directly from authorized manufacturers and distributors. We do not sell counterfeit or third-party replica products. Each item includes authenticity documentation where applicable.

Q: Can I request a product that is not listed on your site?
A: You can submit a product request through our "Suggest a Product" form on the Contact page. Our buying team reviews all suggestions and we may add highly requested items to our catalog.

Q: What does "Pre-order" mean?
A: Pre-order means the item is not yet in stock but is available to reserve. Your card will be charged at the time of purchase and the item will ship as soon as it arrives at our warehouse. Estimated arrival dates are shown on the product page.
