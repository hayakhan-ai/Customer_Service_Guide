ORDERS AND SHIPPING - CUSTOMER SUPPORT GUIDE

Q: How do I place an order?
A: To place an order, browse our website and add items to your cart. Once ready, click the cart icon, review your items, and proceed to checkout. Enter your shipping address and payment details, then confirm your order. You will receive a confirmation email within a few minutes.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days. Express shipping takes 2–3 business days. Overnight shipping is available for orders placed before 12:00 PM and arrives the next business day. Shipping times may vary during holidays and peak seasons.

Q: How much does shipping cost?
A: Standard shipping costs $4.99. Express shipping costs $9.99. Overnight shipping costs $19.99. Orders above $50 qualify for free standard shipping automatically.

Q: Can I track my order?
A: Yes. Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number on our website under "Track My Order" or visit the courier's website directly to see real-time updates.

Q: Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
A: Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Please contact our support team immediately at support@ourstore.com if you need to make changes.

Q: My order says delivered but I didn't receive it. What should I do?
A: First check around your door, with neighbors, or your building's mail room. If still not found, wait 24 hours as deliveries are sometimes marked early. If the issue persists, contact us within 7 days of the delivery date and we will investigate with the courier.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to over 30 countries. International shipping takes 10–20 business days depending on the destination. International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the customer.

Q: What carriers do you use?
A: We use UPS, FedEx, and USPS depending on your location and selected shipping method. The carrier will be shown in your shipping confirmation email.
